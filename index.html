About

Comfortable on both sides of the data.

I like problems where the data is messy and the product has to be real: scrapers that survive layout changes, schemas that reconcile four different sources, and interfaces that make the result obvious.

My background spans data engineering, NLP/LLM tooling and web product work. I care about reproducible pipelines, clean data contracts and the unglamorous details — compliance, timezones, currencies, retries and edge cases — that decide whether something actually works.

These projects are personal builds, but they follow the same rule I use at work: ship the whole loop, from ingestion to the screen.
